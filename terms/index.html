Performative isn't medical, fitness or mental-health advice, and goals and challenges aren't a training plan. Exercise and study within your limits, rest when you need to, and check with a doctor before starting something new if you have a health condition. Rest days never break your streak.

Be aware of where you are when you take photos. Don't take them while driving or cycling, or anywhere that isn't safe or allowed.
