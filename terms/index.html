Challenges and rewards

  • We run every challenge. Performative Group organises and promotes every challenge on Performative, including challenges sponsored by brands.
  • Rewards come from the brand. Some sponsored challenges offer a reward for finishing (such as a discount code), a prize for the top 3, or both. The brand supplies the reward and honours it when you use it with them. We email it only to the people who earn it, and we don't give the brand your name or contact details.
  • Free to join. No purchase is necessary to join a challenge or earn a reward.
  • No prize draws. Rewards are never given by chance. A reward for finishing goes to everyone who does the number of days the challenge asks for. A top-3 prize goes to the three people with the most days done; ties go to the most minutes timed in the app (counting up to 3 hours a day), then to whoever joined first.
  • Checking winners. Before sending top-3 prizes, we check the winners' posts. Anyone whose posts don't show the activity, or who breaks these terms, can be disqualified, and the next person moves up.
  • Official rules. Each challenge with a reward has its own official rules, shown in the app before you join. They say who can take part, the dates, how activity is counted, what the reward is and how it's delivered.
  • Fair play. Results are worked out from your timed sessions in the app and, for distance and step challenges, your Apple Health workouts and steps. Our count is final. We may remove anyone who cheats.
  • Apple is not a sponsor of, and is not involved in any way with, any challenge or reward.

When Plus ends

If your subscription ends (you cancel, a payment fails, or it's refunded), your account goes back to the free plan. You don't lose your posts, photos, friends or history. Where you're using more than the free plan allows, the app brings things back within the free limits and tells you exactly what changed:

  • Goals longer than a week, or ongoing goals, end a week from that day.
  • If you have more than 3 daily goals running, the newest ones end.
  • Groups you started with more than 10 people are closed, along with their chats and group goals.
  • If you're in more than 5 groups, you leave the ones you joined longest ago.
  • If you're in more than 2 challenges, you leave the extra ones. Days you've already done stay on your profile.

If you subscribe again, the higher limits come back straight away, but things that were closed or left aren't restored automatically.
