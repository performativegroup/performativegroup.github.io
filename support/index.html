Support

Answers to common questions, and a real person when you need one.

Email us: support@performativegroup.com
We usually reply within one working day (Hong Kong time).

Using the app

Why can't I post a photo from my camera roll?

Every post is a live photo taken at the end of what you did, so a post always means you actually did it.

My streak broke on a rest day

Rest days never break your streak. Check your rest days in Goals; days you've marked as rest show as dim squares.

My run or steps didn't come through

Make sure Apple Health access is on: open the iPhone's Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices › Performative, and allow workouts and steps. Then pull down to refresh in the app.

I'm not getting notifications

Open the iPhone's Settings › Notifications › Performative and turn on Allow Notifications. In the app's Settings, you can choose exactly which reminders you get.

Your account

How do I make my account private?

Go to Profile › Settings › Privacy and turn on Private account. Only people you approve will see your posts.

How do I reset my password?

On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot password and enter your email. We'll send you a 6-digit code.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Profile › Settings and tap Delete account at the bottom. More about deleting your account.

Safety

How do I report a post, message or person?

Tap ••• on the post or profile and choose Report. You can report a single chat message too. We review every report within 24 hours. You can also block someone from the same menu.

Someone might be in danger

Report it in the app straight away. In an emergency in Hong Kong, call 999.

Delete your account

In the app: go to Profile › Settings › Delete account (at the bottom). Your account, posts, photos, chats, goals and challenge entries are removed from our servers straight away and can't be recovered.

Can't open the app? Email support@performativegroup.com from the email address on your account, and we'll delete it within 7 days.

Copies in our backups are deleted automatically within 30 days. Sponsors only ever get counts and totals, so nothing they have is about you. See our Privacy Policy for how long we keep things.
