What's inside

Built to keep you going.

Weekly and personal goals

Set how often you want to do each thing. Start a personal goal, like a week of journalling, and fill in its squares.

Friends and groups

See what your friends got done today. Make a group for your team, class or flat and keep each other honest.

Challenges

Join a challenge with people near you or around the world. Read 30 days, run every day for a week, and see how you compare.

Widgets and Lock Screen

Your streak and this week's goals on your Home Screen, and your timer live on the Lock Screen while you work.

Apple Health

Runs, rides, swims and steps come straight from your Apple Health workouts, so the numbers are real.

Focus lock

Leave the app mid-session and you get a short warning to come back. Stay with it, and it counts.

Questions

Good to know.

When can I get it?

Performative is coming soon to the App Store for iPhone. It needs iOS 17 or later.

Is it free?

Yes. Everything you need is free. Performative Plus is an optional monthly subscription with room for more goals, groups and challenges.

Why can't I post from my camera roll?

Because a post should mean you actually did it. Every post is a live photo taken at the end of what you did, straight from the camera.

Who can see my posts?

You choose for each post: friends, a group or everyone. You can also make your whole account private, so only people you approve can follow along.

Is it on Android?

Not yet. We're starting with iPhone.

What languages does it speak?

English, Simplified Chinese and Traditional Chinese.

How old do I need to be?

13 or older. If you're under 18, a parent or guardian needs to agree to the Terms.
