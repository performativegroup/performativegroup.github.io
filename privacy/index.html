Privacy

Performative is about doing the thing, not being tracked. Here's exactly what we keep, why, and how to delete it.

Last updated 31 October 2026

What we collect

  • Your account: your name, username, email address and profile photo.
  • What you post: the photo, the activity, how long it took, your caption and the time you posted it. If a post comes from an Apple Health workout, the numbers that go with it, such as distance or steps.
  • Your goals and progress: your goals, rest days, streaks and the challenges you join.
  • Your connections: friends, groups, likes, comments and chats.
  • Safety: reports and blocks you make, and reports about your content.
  • Your country, if you allow it: if you let Performative use your approximate location, we save only your country, so we can show challenges that are open where you are. We never save where you are.
  • Crash logs, so we can fix problems.

What we don't do

  • We never sell your data, and we don't use it for advertising tracking.
  • Location is removed from every photo before it's posted, and the app never tracks where you go.
  • Posts only use the live camera. Your photo library is used only for the profile photo you pick.
  • Apple Health data is read only when you choose to link a workout or steps, and is never shared with anyone else.

Who sees what

You choose who sees each post: your friends, a group, or everyone. If your account is private, only people you approve can see your posts. Your email address is never shown to other people.

Sponsored challenges

Some challenges are sponsored by brands. Sponsors only ever receive totals, such as how many people joined and the total distance covered. They never receive your name, photos, posts, messages or contact details. Countries with fewer than 5 people taking part are grouped together.

Where your data is kept

Your data is stored with our hosting provider, Supabase, in Tokyo, Japan, and is sent over encrypted connections. Performative is run from Hong Kong.

Your choices

  • Make your account private, turn on quiet mode, or block people, from Settings.
  • Turn location, notifications and Apple Health on or off at any time in the iPhone's Settings.
  • Delete your account, and everything in it, from Settings in the app. It's removed from our servers and can't be recovered.

Children

You must be 13 or older to use Performative. If you're under 18, a parent or guardian must agree to the Terms.

Changes

If we change how we use your data, we'll update this page and tell you in the app before it takes effect.

Questions about your data, or want a copy of it? Email support@performativegroup.com.
