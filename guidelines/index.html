Community Guidelines

Performative works because every post is real and everyone feels safe enough to show up. These are the rules that keep it that way, for every post, photo, caption, comment, chat, profile and group.

Last updated 19 November 2026

Post the real thing

  • Live and yours. Posts are live photos of something you actually did. Don't post old photos, screenshots, someone else's photo, or images made or changed with AI or editing apps to look like something you did.
  • Honest numbers. Don't fake or inflate durations, distances, steps, streaks or challenge progress, or leave a timer running on something you didn't do.
  • One of you. One person, one account. Don't pretend to be someone else, including a brand or a famous person, and don't buy, sell or share accounts.
  • Together means together. Only add friends to a post if you really did it with them.

Children are off limits

We have zero tolerance for anything that sexualises, exploits or endangers a child. That includes images, drawings or text; grooming; asking a young person for photos, personal details or to meet; and sextortion. We remove it straight away, close every account involved and report it to the police or the relevant child-protection body. We may also act on this kind of behaviour when it happens off Performative.

Adults: don't try to start conversations with teenagers you don't know in real life. Chats on Performative are only between friends and people in the same group, and accounts of under-18s are always private.

Never allowed

  • Nudity and sexual content, sexual messages or requests, and sharing or threatening to share intimate images of anyone. Gym and swim photos are fine; posing to be sexual isn't.
  • Threats and violence, including wishing harm on someone, gory images, cruelty to animals, and praising or supporting terrorists, violent extremists or organised crime.
  • Encouraging self-harm, suicide or eating disorders, including "thinspiration", extreme calorie targets, purging, and dangerous challenges or stunts.
  • Bullying and harassment, including body-shaming, mocking someone's progress, piling on, or telling someone to hurt themselves.
  • Hate, meaning attacks on people for their race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, caste, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability or serious illness, and hateful symbols or slurs.
  • Illegal goods and activity, including drugs, steroids and prescription medicines, weapons, alcohol or tobacco aimed at under-18s, gambling, and anything else that's against the law where you are.
  • Scams and fraud, including fake giveaways, phishing links, investment schemes and asking people for money or login details.
  • Dangerous health claims, such as telling people to stop medical treatment, or selling "miracle" supplements and cures.
  • Spam, including fake likes, follow-for-follow schemes, repeated messages, and selling things.

Respect people's privacy

  • Don't share anyone's private information, like their address, phone number, email, ID documents, health details, or where they'll be.
  • Check you're allowed to take photos where you are. Never take photos in changing rooms, toilets or anywhere people expect privacy.
  • Keep strangers out of shot as best you can, and never post a child who isn't yours without their parent's permission.
  • If someone asks you to take down a photo of them, please do. If you won't, they can report it to us.

Only post what's yours

Only post photos and words you have the right to share. If you think something on Performative uses your copyright or trademark without permission, email support@performativegroup.com with the details listed in our Terms. Accounts that keep posting other people's work are closed.

Brands and ads

Sponsored challenges are run by us and labelled "Sponsored". If you post about something you were paid, gifted or given a discount for, say so clearly in your caption (for example "#ad" or "gifted"). Don't use Performative to sell things.

Look after yourself and each other

Talking about your own journey, including setbacks, injuries and recovery, is welcome. Keep it non-graphic, and share your experience rather than medical advice. If someone seems to be in danger, report their post or profile and we'll look at it first. In an emergency, call your local emergency number: 999 in Hong Kong and the UK, 911 in the US, 000 in Australia, or 112 in the EU. If you're struggling, you can find free, confidential support near you at findahelpline.com.

Who can join

You must be 13 or older, or older where the law where you live says so (for example 16 in Australia). If you're under 18, a parent or guardian must agree to the Terms. If you think someone is under the minimum age, report their profile.

How we enforce

  • Reports. Anyone can report a post, comment, message or profile from the ••• menu, or email us, even without an account. We review reports within 24 hours, and the most serious ones first.
  • Automatic checks. The app blocks or stars out words that break these guidelines, and a post reported by 3 or more people is hidden until we've looked at it. A person always makes the final decision.
  • What we do. Depending on how serious it is, we remove the content, give a strike, limit features (like joining challenges, appearing on leaderboards or posting), or suspend or close the account. A strike lasts 12 months, and 3 strikes in 12 months closes the account. Anything that harms a child, a credible threat to someone's life, or terrorist content closes the account on the first breach and is reported to the authorities.
  • The same rules for everyone. These guidelines apply to every account, including ours and sponsors'.
  • We tell you why. If we act on your content or account, we'll tell you what rule it broke, unless that would put someone at risk.
  • Appeals. If you think we got it wrong, email us within 30 days. We'll look at it again from the start, and reply within 14 days. If we got it wrong, we'll put things back.
  • No way back in. If we close your account, don't make a new one.
To report something, tap ••• on the post or profile in the app. You can also email support@performativegroup.com.
