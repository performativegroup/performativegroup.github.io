Community Guidelines

Performative works because every post is real. These are the rules that keep it that way.

Last updated 31 October 2026

Post the real thing

Photos must be live shots of what you did. Don't fake durations, streaks or challenge progress, and don't pretend to be someone else.

Never allowed

Nudity or sexual content. Violence, threats or encouraging self-harm. Harassment, bullying or body-shaming. Hate speech or hateful symbols. Illegal activity, drugs or weapons. Dangerous challenges or extreme dieting. Other people's private information. Spam, scams or selling. Content you don't have the rights to.

Take care

Check you're allowed to take photos where you are, and keep strangers out of shot. Share your own journey, not medical advice. If someone might be in danger, report it, and in an emergency in Hong Kong call 999.

Who can join

You must be 13 or older. If you're under 18, a parent or guardian must agree to the Terms.

How we enforce

Anyone can report or block. We review reports within 24 hours. Posts reported by 3 or more people are hidden until reviewed. Serious breaches get a strike; 3 strikes in 12 months closes the account. Anything involving a child or a threat to life is removed at once and reported to the authorities. You can appeal a decision once within 30 days.

To report something, tap ••• on the post or profile in the app. You can also email support@performativegroup.com.
