Community Guidelines

Performative works because every post is real. These are the rules that keep it that way.

Last updated 1 November 2026

Post the real thing

Photos must be live shots of what you did. Don't fake durations, streaks, steps or challenge progress, don't use more than one account, and don't pretend to be someone else.

Never allowed

  • Anything that sexualises or endangers a child, including grooming or asking a young person for photos or to meet. We remove it at once, close the account and report it to the authorities.
  • Nudity or sexual content, including sharing intimate images of anyone.
  • Violence and threats, including supporting terrorism or violent groups.
  • Encouraging self-harm, suicide or eating disorders, including extreme dieting, purging or dangerous challenges.
  • Harassment, bullying or body-shaming, and hate speech or hateful symbols.
  • Illegal activity, including buying or selling drugs, weapons or other regulated goods.
  • Other people's private information, like addresses, phone numbers or where someone will be.
  • Spam and scams, including fake likes, follow-for-follow schemes, links to scams, and selling things.
  • Content you don't have the rights to, like someone else's photos.

Respect the people around you

Check you're allowed to take photos where you are, and keep strangers out of shot, especially other people's children. Never take photos in changing rooms or toilets. If someone asks you to remove a photo of them, please do.

Brands and ads

Sponsored challenges are run by us and labelled "Sponsored". If you post about something you were paid or gifted for, say so in your caption (for example "#ad").

Take care

Share your own journey, not medical advice. If someone might be in danger, report it. In an emergency, call your local emergency number: 999 in Hong Kong and the UK, 911 in the US, or 112 in the EU. If you're struggling, you can find free, confidential support near you at findahelpline.com.

Who can join

You must be 13 or older, or older if the law where you live says so. If you're under 18, a parent or guardian must agree to the Terms.

How we enforce

Anyone can report or block. We review reports within 24 hours. Posts reported by 3 or more people are hidden until reviewed, and a person always makes the final decision. Serious breaches get a strike; 3 strikes in 12 months closes the account. Anything involving a child or a threat to life is removed at once and reported to the authorities. You can appeal a decision once within 30 days by emailing us, and we'll reply within 14 days.

To report something, tap ••• on the post or profile in the app. You can also email support@performativegroup.com.
