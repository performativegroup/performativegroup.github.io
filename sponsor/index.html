Before you start

What you'll need.

It takes about five minutes. You can see prices without filling anything in.

An idea

What people do each day, like running 3 km, reading 20 minutes or 8,000 steps.

Your dates

How long it runs, from 3 to 60 days, starting at least 5 days from now.

Your brand

A square logo (PNG or JPEG) and your brand colour. We'll check it reads well.

Rewards, if you like

Optional, but they help sign-ups: a discount code for everyone who finishes, a prize for the top 3, or both. Rewards go on timed challenges.

Questions

Good to know.

Is this page just for brands?

Yes. Sponsoring is for companies and organisations that want to run a branded challenge. If you just want to use Performative, head to the main site.

How is the price worked out?

It depends on two things you choose: how many days it runs and how many spots it has. A 7-day challenge with 50 spots is US$64, and the price grows gently from there, so doubling the days or the spots costs well under double (14 days with 100 spots is about US$120; 30 days with 500 spots is about US$345). Featuring it on an area page or the main page adds a flat fee. The participant estimate on the quote page is a guide to how many might join, based on how many people are on Performative where it's open.

What data do I get about participants?

Counts and totals only: how many joined, posted and stayed in, live photos posted, and which countries people are in. For timed challenges you also see the hours logged in the app and how many people stayed on track. We never share names, photos, posts, messages, contact details or anyone's Apple Health data, so for distance and step challenges you see joins and posts only, never distances, step counts or who hit a target. Countries with fewer than 5 people are grouped together.

Can I change it after sending?

Yes. Reply to our email before you pay and we'll update it. After it's live, the wording and dates are fixed so it's fair to everyone taking part.

Can I offer a reward?

Yes, two kinds, and you can offer either, both or neither. A reward for finishing: you choose how many days people need to do, and everyone who gets there is emailed your code the morning after it ends. A prize for the top 3: the three people with the most days done win; ties go to the most minutes logged (counting up to 3 hours a day), then to whoever joined first. We check the top 3's posts before anyone wins, then email each winner the code or claim instructions you gave us. Either way, you never receive anyone's personal details. Rewards can only go on challenges measured by the app's timer, not distance or steps. Winning is by effort, never by chance, and nobody has to buy anything to take part. The app shows the official rules before anyone joins.

What won't you run?

Challenges for alcohol, tobacco or vaping, gambling, weight-loss products or medical claims, anything unsafe (for example extreme fasting or distances without preparation), and anything misleading. We may decline any request.

Sponsor terms

The short version.

  1. Sending a request doesn't commit you to anything, and nothing is charged until we approve it and you pay our invoice.
  2. Prices are in US dollars and are fixed once we approve your request. Every challenge has a set number of spots, from 50 to 5,000. The estimated participants are a guide, not a guarantee.
  3. You confirm you have the right to use the name, logo and colours you send us.
  4. Performative Group organises and promotes every challenge, and writes the official rules shown in the app. Rewards are optional: a reward for everyone who finishes, a prize for the top 3, or both. You supply them at your own cost and honour them as described, and you send us the codes or claim instructions before the challenge starts. We email them only to the people who earn them; top-3 places are decided by days done, then minutes (up to 3 hours a day), then who joined first, after we check the top 3's posts. Rewards can only go on challenges measured by the app's timer, and we don't run prize draws or games of chance.
  5. We may edit wording for clarity, and we may decline any request. If we have to stop a challenge early because of a problem on our side, we refund the unused share.
  6. We share counts and totals only. You won't receive personal data or any Apple Health data about anyone taking part.
