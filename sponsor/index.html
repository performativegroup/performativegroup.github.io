Before you start

What you'll need.

An idea

What people do each day, like running 3 km, reading 20 minutes or 8,000 steps.

Your dates

How long it runs, from 3 to 60 days, starting at least 5 days from now.

Your brand

A square logo (PNG or JPEG) and your brand colour. We'll check it reads well.

A prize, if you like

Optional, but it helps sign-ups: a discount for finishers, or a prize for the top three.

Questions

Good to know.

Is this page just for brands?

Yes. Sponsoring is for companies and organisations that want to run a branded challenge. If you just want to use Performative, head to the main site.

How is the price worked out?

We start from how many people are on Performative where your challenge is open, and how many typically join a challenge. We adjust for how long it runs (longer challenges get fewer sign-ups but more days of activity), featured placement, and how popular your chosen activities are with those people right now. There's a minimum price, and spots you limit are never charged beyond the limit.

What data do I get about participants?

Totals only: how many joined, took part, stayed on track, the total activity, live photos posted and which countries people are in. We never share names, photos, posts, messages or contact details. Countries with fewer than 5 people are grouped together.

Can I change it after sending?

Yes. Reply to our email before you pay and we'll update it. After it's live, the wording and dates are fixed so it's fair to everyone taking part.

Can I give a prize?

Yes, and it helps sign-ups. Tell us when you build it. Prizes are handled with you during the review, so winners can choose to share their details with you.

What won't you run?

Challenges for alcohol, tobacco or vaping, gambling, weight-loss products or medical claims, anything unsafe (for example extreme fasting or distances without preparation), and anything misleading. We may decline any request.

Sponsor terms

The short version.

  1. Sending a request doesn't commit you to anything, and nothing is charged until we approve it and you pay our invoice.
  2. Prices are in Hong Kong dollars and are fixed once we approve your request. The estimated participants are a guide, not a guarantee.
  3. You confirm you have the right to use the name, logo and colours you send us, and that any prize you offer will be provided as described.
  4. We may edit wording for clarity, and we may decline any request. If we have to stop a challenge early because of a problem on our side, we refund the unused share.
  5. We share totals only. You won't receive personal data about anyone taking part.
